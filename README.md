# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** designed to provide a clear overview of product sales performance across different **months, regions, and product categories**.

## 🚀 Dashboard Overview

This dashboard helps users analyze sales data through interactive filters and visualizations. It provides insights into total sales, revenue, monthly trends, regional performance, and product-level contribution.

### ✨ Key Features

- 📅 **Date Filter** – Analyze sales across different time periods and months.
- 🌍 **Regional Analysis** – Compare sales performance across East, North, South, and West regions.
- 🛍️ **Product Analysis** – Track performance of:
  - Keyboard
  - Monitor
  - Mouse
  - Printer
  - Scanner
  - Speaker
- 💰 **Total Amount** – Displays the overall sales amount.
- 📈 **Total Sales** – Shows the total number of sales transactions.
- 📊 **Monthly Sales Trend** – Visualizes sales performance month by month.
- 🌎 **Sales by Region** – Compares total sales across different regions.
- 🏆 **Product Contribution** – Shows the percentage contribution of each product to overall sales.
- 🎛️ **Interactive Filters** – Users can filter the dashboard by date, region, and product.

## 📈 Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

1. **KPI Cards** – Total Amount and Total Sales
2. **Sales by Products** – Product-wise percentage contribution
3. **Sales by Months** – Monthly sales trend
4. **Sales by Region** – Regional sales comparison
5. **Interactive Slicers** – Date, Region, and Product filters

## 🎯 Purpose

The primary goal of this dashboard is to transform raw sales data into **actionable business insights**. It enables users to quickly identify sales trends, compare regional performance, and understand which products contribute most to overall revenue.
